# PSEGroupAssignment: BabySure
This project is part of our assignment under the module "Principles of Software Engineering" at Taylor's University. The application's prototype supports Good Health and Well-Being (SDG 3). BabySure is a mobile-based app that links to a dedicated physical device. This device is a 360° camera with a speaker, built-in thermistor, and microphone attached. 

With our product, parents can constantly monitor the baby anytime and anywhere with their smartphone. Our product aims to help parents who are unable to be physically present with their baby due to a hectic lifestyle.
